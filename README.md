The clear coat is responsible for giving your car its glossy finish and providing a barrier against UV radiation, moisture, and minor abrasion. NanoPolix targets this specific layer, working to smooth and refine it without penetrating into the coloured paint beneath.

The result is a smoother surface that reflects light more uniformly, which is what produces the appearance of a restored, glossy finish. This is a mechanical polishing process — the cloth physically abrades the surface at a microscopic level rather than chemically altering or filling the imperfections.
It is important to understand what NanoPolix is specifically formulated to address. Once you have worked over the treated area with the NanoPolix cloth, the finishing stage involves buffing the surface with a separate, clean microfiber towel.
